About Boycon

Reliable Electrical Panel Boards, Built With Purpose.

Boycon Construction Supply and Trading Corporation was established in 2020 to serve contractors, developers, and industrial facilities that needed a dependable source for electrical panel boards and related supplies. What began as a focused electrical supply store has grown into a company trusted for the design, fabrication, and assembly of panel boards across residential, commercial, and industrial projects.

Based in San Pedro, Laguna, we combine hands-on fabrication expertise with a growing catalog of electrical materials and construction-related supplies — giving our clients one dependable source for both custom panel boards and everyday project needs.

Our Services

Full-Service Electrical Panel Board Solutions

From initial design to ongoing maintenance, our services cover the full lifecycle of your electrical panel board and equipment needs.

Panel Board Design

We prepare single-line diagrams and detailed panel schedules based on your load requirements, ready for your review and approval before fabrication begins.

Panel Board Fabrication

Our in-house fabrication team builds distribution panels, MDBs, MCCs, and ATS panels to spec, with quality checks at every stage.

Electrical Supply

We supply wires, cables, breakers, and a wide range of industrial and construction electrical materials for projects of any scale.

Electrical Installation Support

Our team can assist your electricians during installation, including initial panel energization and coordination on site.

Industrial Electrical Solutions

From motor control centers to power distribution equipment, we support the electrical systems of manufacturing and processing facilities.

Custom Panel Manufacturing

No two projects are the same — we fabricate panels to non-standard sizes, enclosure types, and configurations as needed.

Preventive Maintenance

Scheduled inspection and testing services to keep your panel boards and electrical systems running safely.

Technical Consultation

Not sure what your project needs? Our team can walk you through panel sizing, load calculation, and equipment selection.

Need Help With a Specific Requirement?

Tell us about your project and we'll recommend the right panel board or service for it.
