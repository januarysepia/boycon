About Boycon

Reliable Electrical Panel Boards, Built With Purpose.

Boycon Construction Supply and Trading Corporation was established in 2020 to serve contractors, developers, and industrial facilities that needed a dependable source for electrical panel boards and related supplies. What began as a focused electrical supply store has grown into a company trusted for the design, fabrication, and assembly of panel boards across residential, commercial, and industrial projects.

Based in San Pedro, Laguna, we combine hands-on fabrication expertise with a growing catalog of electrical materials and construction-related supplies — giving our clients one dependable source for both custom panel boards and everyday project needs.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions about our products, panel boards, quotations, and services.

What types of electrical panel boards do you offer?

We offer distribution panels, main distribution boards, motor control centers, and automatic transfer switch panels, all available as standard configurations or custom-built to your specifications.

Do you offer custom panel board fabrication?

Yes. Most of our panel boards are custom-fabricated based on your load schedule, available space, and project requirements. Send us your details and we'll prepare a design for your approval.

What is the typical lead time for a custom panel board?

Lead time depends on the type and complexity of the panel, but most custom panel boards are completed within 1 to 3 weeks from design approval. We'll give you a specific timeline once we review your requirements.

Do you provide free quotations?

Yes, quotations are free. Simply fill out our Get a Quote form or contact us directly with your project details, and our team will prepare a detailed quotation.

What areas do you deliver to?

We regularly deliver across CALABARZON and Metro Manila. For projects outside these areas, please reach out so we can confirm delivery arrangements and costs.

Do you offer installation support?

Yes, our team can support your electricians during installation and initial panel energization. Installation support can be included as part of your quotation upon request.

What warranty do you provide on panel boards?

Panel boards come with a workmanship warranty covering fabrication defects, in addition to any manufacturer warranty on individual components such as breakers. Specific warranty terms are included with every quotation.

Can you supply materials only, without fabrication?

Yes. We supply electrical wires, cables, breakers, and industrial electrical materials on their own, separate from panel board fabrication services.

Are your panel boards compliant with the Philippine Electrical Code?

Yes, our panel board fabrication follows the Philippine Electrical Code and standard industry practices to help ensure your project passes inspection.

How do I request a quote?

You can use our Get a Quote page, call us at 0936 043 4193, or email obeltran.boyconph@gmail.com with your project details. We'll respond with a detailed quotation as soon as possible.
